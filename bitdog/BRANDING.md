# Bitcoin Dog branding

Source of truth: https://bit-dog-coin.netlify.app/ (checked September 29, 2026).

The project header says **Bitcoin Dog**, title case, without a trailing period. Its mark is the orange Bitcoin coin, not a cartoon dog head. `icon.svg` copies the exact SVG from the website header and is used for the game header and favicon.

Header styling matches the source: Baloo 2, weight 700, 20 px; text #3B2A1A; 30 × 30 px mark; 10 px gap; letter spacing -0.01em. Coin orange is #F7931A; the symbol is #191204. Do not replace this lockup with a newly generated mascot or an invented wordmark.

BitDog: Zoomies remains the game title. The golden retriever, black collar and gold BIT tag remain the in-game character. GameSlop credit and themed gameplay scenery are separate from the project logo.

The fetched website snapshot and visual comparison are saved under `artifacts/bitdog/`. No website scripts or token purchase controls are imported into the game.
