HOW TO PLAY

Good dog.
Go fetch.

Big zoomies. Hidden shortcuts. One very good boy on a Bitcoin adventure.

01 / Fetch it. Bring it home.

Explore the level and find the big Bitcoin at the far end. Take your time on the way out: the countdown starts when you grab it. Then turn around and race back to your owner before time runs out.

Play all eight levels in order with five hearts for the entire run. A collision can cost a heart, and hearts do not refill between levels. Lose all five or run out of return time to end the run. A new run starts at level 1 with five hearts.

02 / Give it a roll.

Some obstacles can be broken by rolling through them for bonus points. Others won’t budge. Build up speed before you roll, experiment, and jump or steer clear of anything that stops you.

Which ones break? That’s for a curious dog to discover. Each obstacle you break earns 1,000 bonus points, once per attempt.

03 / Keep your momentum.

Hold Sprint while running to build speed. Hold Roll to curl up and coast. Movement keys won’t propel you while rolling: you slow to a stop on flat ground, gain speed downhill and slow faster uphill.

Release Roll to run again. Hold Jump for a higher leap; release it early for a shorter hop. Look for higher routes and shortcuts—there’s more than one way to fetch.

04 / Make the fetch count.

  • Each collected coin100 points
  • Each obstacle broken1,000 points
  • Each whole second left on delivery50 points
  • Bringing the big Bitcoin home1,000 points

Balance collecting coins with a speedy return. Deliver without losing a heart in that level for a Perfect Fetch. Your points add up across the run.

Your paws. Your controls.

These are the default keyboard controls. Open Controls Setup on the title or pause screen to change them. Your choices save on this device.

Run
A / D or left / right arrows
Jump
Space, W or up arrow
Sprint
Hold Shift while running
Roll
Hold S or down arrow
Pause / resume
Esc or P
Skip the intro
Enter or the skip button

On mobile: use the thumb joystick to run and the separate Jump, Sprint and Roll buttons. You can use the stick and an action button together.
