# Slop in Time — 16-bit art upgrade

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. All eight final assets are saved in `games/slop-in-time/skin/gameslop/` as `city-pixel-v2.png`, `pirate-pixel-v2.png`, `jungle-pixel-v2.png`, `temple-pixel-v2.png`, `west-pixel-v2.png`, `future-pixel-v2.png`, `mascot-pixel-v2.png`, and `enemies-pixel-v2.png`.

Each environment contains three authored horizontal sections. The renderer assembles these into one 4,352 × 340 panorama with fixed dithered overlaps. Camera movement scrolls that continuous panorama behind the full-speed ground plane. The world renders at 480 × 270 and scales without smoothing; the HUD remains crisp at 960 × 540. The mascot and enemies are redrawn from the original project atlases, preserving their identities. The boss red palette is a precomputed runtime effect.
