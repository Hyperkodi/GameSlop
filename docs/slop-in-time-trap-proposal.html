GAMESLOP / SLOP IN TIME / APPROVED DESIGN

Traps that belong in their world.

Six approved environmental layouts. Implemented with only three widely spaced hazard sites per level. These are reference schematics; reserve ideas remain excluded.

Replace the shared wrecking balls and generic crushers with the mechanisms below. Use one hazard at a time when teaching it, then combine only after the player understands both. Keep bosses and their established powers.

ERA 01

Neon Afterhours

Street infrastructure, not construction-site hazards

Arcade Row → service alley → canal walkway → substation

Ruptured steam main

A wall pipe feeds a pavement grate. A pressure gauge rises and the grate hisses for 1 second before soft steam blocks one depth lane.

Counterplay: Walk around the cloud; turn the hydrant valve to vent away from the street.

Live cable and puddle

A damaged power cabinet has a visible cable lying in rainwater. Sparks trace the cable before the puddle pulses.

Counterplay: Use the dry lane or break the cabinet cutoff to stop the cycle.

Interactive scenery
Hydrant valve redirects steam • parked scooter can be kicked into a rolling attack.

Reserve idea: an arcade sign falling from visibly broken wall brackets. Avoid using it until the basic hazards read clearly.

ERA 02

Dead Man’s Dock

Ship fittings and harbor machinery

Wharf → net market → lower pier → cannon quay → captain’s ship

Deck-mounted cannon

The cannon sits on a wheeled carriage bolted to the pier. Its fuse burns and muzzle smokes before a cannonball crosses one lane.

Counterplay: Jump the low cannonball or change lanes; roll a barrel into the gun carriage to disable it.

Cargo sling

A visible dock crane, pulley, rope and net hold the load. Rope creaks and a moving shadow marks its short swing arc.

Counterplay: Use the outside lane. Strike the capstan release to drop the net onto enemies once.

Interactive scenery
Capstan releases the hanging net • loose powder barrel rolls across the deck when struck.

Reserve idea: a broken plank that buckles before opening. Keep holes rare and visibly connected to damaged decking.

ERA 03

Primordial Punch

Rock, water, roots and animal territory

Fern trail → ravine → waterfall bend → basalt crossing → caldera

Cliff rockfall

Loose stones sit in a visible cracked outcrop. Pebbles fall first, then ground shadows show where larger rocks will land.

Counterplay: Move around the marked impacts; the rocks can hit ordinary enemies too.

Thermal geyser

A mineral-ringed pool bubbles and swells before a translucent plume rises from its vent.

Counterplay: Take the dry edge. A kickable stone slab can cover one vent and stop it.

Interactive scenery
Rotten log rolls downhill into enemies • loose stone slab plugs a geyser.

Reserve idea: a snapping plant rooted in a fern bed. It would telegraph by closing its leaves, never appear in bare paving.

ERA 04

Moonlit Shogunate

Deliberate castle defenses and shrine mechanisms

Shrine approach → garden → bridge → castle gate → courtyard

Guardian dart slots

Stone guardian mouths face across a pressure-tile lane. The tile sinks with a click and the mouths glint before darts fire.

Counterplay: Step off the tile, change lanes or jump the low darts.

Winch-driven portcullis

A castle gate sits inside two posts, with chains running to a visible winch. It rattles before dropping into one crossing.

Counterplay: Wait for the lift or use the side passage. Strike the winch lock to hold it open.

Interactive scenery
Shrine bell on a timber frame stuns enemies • gate winch can lock the portcullis open.

Reserve idea: a supported bamboo water trap. Keep magic exclusive to enemies and the boss rather than unexplained machinery.

ERA 05

Last Train to Trouble

Railway hazards with visible tracks and pressure lines

Main Street → depot → maintenance crossing → trestle → roundhouse

Loose mine cart

Rails connect the coal chute to the crossing. A bell and moving shadow warn before a loaded cart rolls through.

Counterplay: Jump the low cart or cross behind it. The brake lever parks it as cover.

Boiler blow-off

A locomotive boiler connects to a side pipe and floor outlet. The pressure gauge climbs and the valve whistles before venting.

Counterplay: Use the other depth lane; turn the water-tower valve to cool the boiler.

Interactive scenery
Brake lever parks the cart • water valve suppresses the steam cycle.

Reserve idea: a coal-chute spill under a supported loading tower. No floating anvils or unexplained crushing machines.

ERA 06

The Clockwork End

Machines built into the factory, with readable cycles

Assembly line → coolant channel → reactor → observatory → time core

Assembly press

A press hangs from a visible gantry between anchored columns. Amber lamps and descending pistons warn before the head hits the conveyor.

Counterplay: Use the maintenance lane. A console pauses the press for a short safe window.

Reactor arc bridge

Two powered nodes are connected by visible conduits. Charge climbs the nodes and a thin preview line shows the next arc.

Counterplay: Stay off the charged strip; knock a battery canister into the nodes to trip the circuit.

Interactive scenery
Maintenance console pauses a machine cycle • battery canister discharges into a reactor node.

Reserve idea: a conveyor that changes direction. Add only after confirming it works well with the mobile thumbstick.

Approved implementation order

1. Build and playtest the city pipe and cable hazards with their visible source art.
2. Apply that readability standard to the other five eras, using their own mechanisms.
3. Check each warning, escape lane, enemy entrance, camera lock and mobile control combination.
4. Replace the current generic traps only when the themed replacements are ready.

Approval scope: two primary hazards and two interactive scenery objects per era. Reserve ideas stay out of this pass unless requested. Warnings should last at least 0.9 seconds and safe bypasses should remain at least 72 world pixels wide.
