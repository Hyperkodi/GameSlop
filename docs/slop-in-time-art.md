# Slop in Time — production artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Project copies are committed under `games/slop-in-time/skin/gameslop/`:

- `eras-v1.png`: six illustrated environment panels, two columns by three rows.
- `enemies-v1.png`: twelve original enemy poses on a white matte, with authored crops and runtime matte removal.

The enemy atlas was revised once to replace its initially generated gradient background with a uniform white matte. The discarded first image is not used by the game. Runtime sprite crops avoid overlap with neighboring rows, and sprite feet are aligned to the ground plane.
