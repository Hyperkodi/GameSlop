# Slop Survivor v1.6.0 playtest

Wojak faces into the battlefield. Coin, laser and gas use four-frame character sheets; the other eleven weapons use distinct runtime arm/weapon animations made from existing artwork. Projectile visuals originate near the held weapon. Snake section outlines are removed; section damage and retreat remain intact.

Validation: 70 automated tests passed. Chromium portrait/landscape, frozen pause, campaign/tournament save separation, progress preservation and offline reload passed without page errors or failed requests. All 14 weapon poses were rendered for review. Deterministic 45-combination campaign simulation won 42 runs; losses were chapter 13 Hard, 14 Hell and 15 Hell. This is balance evidence, not proof of human difficulty or guaranteed victories.

Physical phone and Telegram in-app testing remain necessary. Tournament scores are local personal bests, not a secured online leaderboard. Eleven attacks use runtime compositing and may benefit from dedicated character art later. No additional art generation is planned for this release.

The previous v1.5.1 release is preserved separately in the workspace. No account credentials, generation scripts or private workspace files are included in this public package.
